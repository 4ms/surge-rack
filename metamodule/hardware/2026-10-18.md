## Limits and evidence

The 46 live cases contain 86,459 processed blocks and 952 overruns.
All patch transfers passed CRC readback; SD failure counters stayed at zero.
All 46 exact patch files produced finite, nonzero output in the host firmware simulator.
No analog hardware audio was captured. Memory headroom, maximum polyphony/unison,
every mode/control setting, Rack-import history behavior, other rates/block sizes
and long-run stability remain unqualified. Prepared maximum-unison tests were
not run after the owner narrowed focus to observed overloads.
Detailed records and raw evidence hashes: [JSON receipt](2026-10-18.json).
The prior patch was restored and detailed profiling disabled.
The unknown-build baseline and first interrupted 0.1.45 attempt are excluded.
The final sweep combines 36 completed cases before a long-command echo timeout
with ten completed remaining cases after that host-parser fix. No individual
measurement spans a failure or reconnect; source sessions remain immutable.

The original raw logs and profiling harness remain in the development workspace;
this PR includes the measured table and artifact hashes, not the USB toolchain.
