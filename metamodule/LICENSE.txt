Surge XT for MetaModule
=======================

This is a port of Surge XT for VCV Rack (https://github.com/surge-synthesizer/surge-rack/)
to the 4ms MetaModule. The port's source is at https://github.com/4ms/surge-rack/.

Artwork
-------

VCV Rack) is copyright Steve and Pierre, and licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/

The images in this plugin are adaptations of that artwork, and are licensed under the same
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license:

  - faceplates/: renderings of the SurgeXTRack panels, using the VCV Rack
	headless screenshot mode with the a mod to hide the controls, jacks, and
	displays
  - xt/*/components/: converted to PNG from the SurgeXTRack component SVGs, and
	in some cases slightly modified
  - metamodule/: PNGs created match the SurgeXTRack controls that are drawn with code
